# Commercial licensing

Garmr's source code is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only** (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).
The AGPL is a strong network-copyleft license: if you run a modified version of
Garmr and let users interact with it over a network, the AGPL requires you to
offer those users the corresponding source of your modified version.

That obligation does not fit every organization. As the copyright holder of the
original Garmr code, **Vetra Automation AB can offer the same code under separate
commercial terms** — for example, to organizations that want to embed or modify
Garmr without the AGPL's source-availability obligations.

## What this document is — and is not

- This is **informational**. It describes that a commercial option exists and how
  to start a conversation about it.
- It is **not** a license grant, an offer, or a contract. **No commercial license
  exists until Vetra Automation AB and the licensee sign a written agreement.**
- Simply contacting Vetra, downloading Garmr, or using it does **not** grant you a
  commercial license or waive any AGPL obligation. Until a commercial agreement is
  executed, your use is governed solely by AGPL-3.0-only.
- Nothing here is legal advice. Obtain your own legal review of how the AGPL
  applies to your deployment.

## Who might want a commercial license

You may want to talk to Vetra if you intend to:

- distribute a modified Garmr, or offer it as a hosted/SaaS service, without
  meeting the AGPL §13 network source-availability requirement;
- embed Garmr (or substantial parts of it) into a proprietary product;
- obtain a warranty, indemnity, support, or SLA that the open-source license
  explicitly disclaims.

If you deploy an **unmodified** Garmr internally and are comfortable with the
AGPL, you generally do **not** need a commercial license — the open-source
license already covers that use.

Open a discussion or contact Vetra Automation AB through the channels listed in
the repository (see the project [`README`](README.md)). Please include:

- your organization and intended use (internal, hosted/SaaS, embedded/OEM);

Vetra will respond with next steps. Terms, pricing, and availability of any
